### Executive Churn Dashboard & Data Storytelling
**Tools: Python, pandas, Tableau** | M.S. Data Analytics Project (D210 - Representation & Reporting)

An interactive Tableau story built for a non-technical executive audience, comparing internal churn data (9,769 customers) with a public competitor dataset (7,043 customers).
 
- Prepared and aligned both datasets in Python, remapping mismatched fields so the two companies could be compared side by side
- Created calculated churn rate and customer lifetime value metrics, finding nearly identical churn (26.50% vs. 26.54%) but a two-year-contract CLV nearly double the competitor's ($6,146 vs. $3,707)
- Designed filters for dataset, contract type, and tenure so leaders could explore the segments relevant to their own areas
- Built for accessibility with colorblind-safe and grayscale palettes, direct labels, and detailed tooltips
